Some of my favorite projects and what I have learned:
Summer Camp Counseling:
I worked with kids from ages 2-12. I learned to collaborate with other counselors at 15 while also following orders from the supervising teachers that taught me a lot of empathy and admiration towards them while also learning to collaborate, negotiate, and storytell as a way to get children to understand the activities, and a LOT of conflict resolution.
Marketing Final Project:
During one my second year fall semester, I was put in a group project where we were tasked with hypothetically creating or improving a product or service and plan its marketing strategy from scratch. We created a brand of fully customizable bras and I took on the roll of project manager. Here I learned how to organize multiple student’s schedules, and trusting my teammates, as it was a project I could not have done all on y own in the span of four months. I also learned how to use excel as I was tasked with the financial end of our strategy for our pitch. I made a preliminary hypothetical production and advertisement budget and ROI for our product for the first four quarters.
HackUTD Axxess Hackathon Preparation:
A week after I was onboarded to the HackUTD team, the axxess hackathon had to be prepared for, and with little to no experience I learned two things. Hope for the best, plan for the worst. Despite being praised by my coworkers for the preparation, the execution of it taught me to be flexible, to have multiple back-up plans, the ability to compromise, knowing the strengths of my teammates and planning with them as a priority.
